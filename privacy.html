Privacy policy

Effective 15 October 2026

ProXR is a web browser and workspace for display glasses, with your iPhone as the controller. It's built to keep what you do to yourself: ProXR has no account, shows no ads, has no analytics and doesn't track you. The developer of ProXR doesn't collect, receive or store any personal information about you.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Browsing. Your tabs, history, sites on Home, downloads and settings are kept in ProXR on your iPhone. Clear your history or website data at any time in Settings › Privacy.
  • Sign-ins. Cookies and website data are kept on your iPhone so sites keep you signed in, as in Safari. Passwords come from your iPhone's Passwords and are filled only into the site they belong to. For a two-step sign-in, ProXR keeps a password in memory for a few minutes so the next page on the same site can fill; it's never saved.
  • Photos and videos. If you allow it, ProXR shows your photos and videos on your glasses. They're read on your iPhone and never sent anywhere. Screen recordings you make are saved to your Photos, or to ProXR's folder in Files if Photos isn't allowed.
  • Voice. Voice typing and voice commands are turned into text on your iPhone. If your iPhone can't understand your language by itself, you can choose to use Apple's speech servers in Settings; only then is what you say sent to Apple, under Apple's privacy policy.
  • Camera and microphone. A website can use them (for a video call, say) only when you allow it, each time it asks.
  • Your free trial. When it started is kept in your iPhone's Keychain, so the trial counts correctly.

What goes over the internet

  • The websites you visit receive what any browser sends them, and their own privacy policies apply. ProXR blocks many ads and trackers by default.
  • Search suggestions. As you type in the address box, what you've typed is sent to the search engine you've chosen (Google, DuckDuckGo, Bing or Brave), to suggest searches. Turn this off in Settings › Search.
  • Site icons are fetched from each site itself, without cookies.
  • Purchases are made with Apple through the App Store. ProXR receives only Apple's record of what was bought on your Apple Account; Apple handles your payment details.
  • The time. During the free trial ProXR asks www.apple.com for the current time, so the trial's 7 days are counted correctly. The request carries no information about you or what you do.

Sharing

ProXR doesn't sell, share or rent information about you, because it doesn't have any. There are no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking libraries in the app.

Children

ProXR isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
