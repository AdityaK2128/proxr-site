Common questions

What do I need?

An iPhone with USB-C (iPhone 15 or later) on iOS 17 or later, and display glasses that plug into it over USB-C, such as XREAL, VITURE, Rokid, RayNeo or TCL NXTWEAR. Monitors and TVs work too.

My glasses show my iPhone's screen instead of ProXR.

Keep ProXR open on your iPhone while you wear your glasses. If your iPhone locks or you switch to another app, the glasses go back to mirroring until you come back. To save battery without locking, use Blackout (the moon button): your iPhone's screen goes dark but keeps working as the trackpad.

If it still mirrors, unplug the glasses and plug them in again with ProXR open.

Things on the glasses are too close to the edge or too small.

Open Settings › Set up for your glasses, or change Edge margin and Interface size in Settings. Both can also be changed on the glasses: swipe down with three fingers.

How do I type and sign in?

Click a text field on the glasses and type with your iPhone's keyboard, or on the keyboard that appears on the glasses. To sign in, tap Passwords above the iPhone's keyboard and pick your account; ProXR fills it into the site on your glasses.

How does the free trial work?

ProXR is free to download. Start the 7-day free trial and every feature is yours for a week. When it ends, browsing in one window stays free, and more windows, pop-out video, Photos and screen recording need ProXR Pro, a one-time purchase. Nothing is charged automatically and there's no subscription.

I bought ProXR Pro. How do I get it on another iPhone?

Sign in with the same Apple Account, then open Settings › ProXR Pro › Restore purchases.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are made through Apple, so refunds are too: go to reportaproblem.apple.com, or write and we'll point you the right way.

A website doesn't work properly.

Open the site's menu on the glasses (two-finger tap) and choose Compatibility mode: the site then loads as it would in Safari. Please write and say which site it was, so it can be fixed.
